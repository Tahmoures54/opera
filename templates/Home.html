Keep engineering, quality, construction and operations aligned around the same project records, responsibilities and acceptance criteria. Follow the project-approved milestone definitions: MC → RFC → RFSU → Operations Acceptance.

Project structure

Organize projects into systems and subsystems, with clear references and weighted progress.

Check sheet execution

Issue inspections, record results, verify independently and preserve signed revisions.

Punch management

Assign deficiencies, track closure and retain verification and waiver history.

Witness & hold points

Schedule notifications, record attendance and manage formal release steps.

Mechanical completion

Track MC, RFC and RFSU milestones with project-specific handover packages.

Progress evidence

Capture dated progress snapshots and keep supporting evidence with each record.

Readiness terminology: RFC means Ready for Commissioning. RFSU means Ready for Start-Up. OPERA issues and stores a dedicated RFC certificate; earlier RF records are migrated automatically. RFSU remains the next start-up readiness milestone. Always follow the approved project handover procedure where terminology differs.
