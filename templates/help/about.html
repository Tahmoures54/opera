About the platform

Integrated pre-commissioning, commissioning and operational handover

OPERA Designed for project teams, contractors, quality teams, client representatives and operations personnel to manage start-up activities from project level down to systems, subsystems and equipment.

OPERA brings check sheet workflows, result capture, independent QC verification, two-party sign-off, punch lists, witness and hold points, mechanical completion, readiness for start-up and handover into one traceable workspace.

OPERA retains the shared identity, database and security foundation while presenting a dedicated product experience focused on commissioning.
